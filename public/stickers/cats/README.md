# Your cat stickers 🐈

Drop pictures of your cats in this folder (PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF).
They show up for everyone in the **Buddha & Portal** sticker pack the next time the site is published (every push to main).

Tips:
- Square pictures look best. Transparent PNGs (with the background removed)
  get a white die-cut sticker outline automatically.
- The file name becomes the sticker name: `mochi-sleepy.png` → "Mochi Sleepy".

You can also make stickers inside the app: tap the sticker button in a chat, then
⭐ → **Make**, choose a cat picture, pick a shape (circle, heart…) and add a caption.
